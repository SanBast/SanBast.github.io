Research.

My research asks how learned representations can tell a model when it is wrong under distribution shift. I work on representation learning, domain generalisation, generative models and retrieval-augmented methods, with a focus on reliable 3D medical image segmentation.

  • Quality control without ground truth

    Telling a segmentation model when it is wrong, across organs, modalities and unseen datasets.

  • Domain generalisation & distribution shift

    Representations that stay reliable when the data changes.

  • Generative models

    Latent diffusion and flow matching to model what a plausible segmentation looks like.

  • Retrieval-augmented & foundation models

    Moving anatomical knowledge from weights into multimodal knowledge bases.

  • 3D & volumetric learning

    Segmentation of CT, MRI and ultrasound volumes.

  • Agentic LLM routing

    Robust routing of LLM agents under non-stationarity and adversarial shift.

TMLR 2026 Journal · First author

Retrieval-Augmented Correction for Generalist Medical Image Segmentation

V. Marcianò, X. Zhang, M. Antonelli, S. Ourselin, M. A. Zuluaga

Moves anatomical knowledge from model weights into a multimodal knowledge base: a single anatomy-agnostic refiner corrects masks across 10 organs and 18 datasets and generalises zero-shot to unseen organs.

Code

NeurIPS 2026 Main conference · First author

MID: Mask-Image Distributional Divergence for Evaluating Medical Image Segmentation

V. Marcianò, X. Zhang, G. Guglielmo, M. Antonelli, S. Ourselin, M. A. Zuluaga

A reference-free, FID-inspired metric comparing joint image–mask distributions in a frozen medical feature space; it ranks segmentation models as Dice does without per-sample labels.

arXiv Code

IEEE TMI 2026 Journal · First author

Diffusion-Based Quality Control of Medical Image Segmentations across Organs

V. Marcianò, H. Chaptoukaev, V. Fernandez, M. J. Cardoso, S. Ourselin, M. Antonelli, M. A. Zuluaga

A self-configuring quality-control framework where a conditional latent diffusion model learns the quality manifold of an organ, evaluated on 15 datasets, 7 organs and 4 imaging techniques.
